## Template Tutorial

The interactive tutorial lives in the **`/tutorial` skill** (`.claude/skills/tutorial/SKILL.md`) — a single source used both during onboarding (when "tutorial" is chosen) and anytime later via `/tutorial`.

When tutorial mode is selected, **read and follow `.claude/skills/tutorial/SKILL.md`**: start by asking whether the user wants a guided tour or to jump straight into the topic menu, keep it interactive and brief, give the design-first and context-management topics extra emphasis, and do **not** scan the repository. At the end, offer to start the real onboarding interview with a chosen shape.
